Reliability and safety details

The watch runs stored alarms and their native repeating rounds without the browser. A precise custom pulse-by-pulse sequence needs this page open, connected, and active. Reloading temporarily disconnects Bluetooth; reconnect is attempted automatically when browser permission is available. A vibration/zap command being accepted is not proof of a physical response. This unofficial interface is still in testing—keep a second wake-up alarm.
